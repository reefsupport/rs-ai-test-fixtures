## Attribution (all 36 images)

All 36 images come from one source: the Roboflow "Aquarium Dataset" (a.k.a. Aquarium Combined; 638 images).

- Author / licensor: Roboflow. The dataset page states the 638 images were "collected by Roboflow from two aquariums in the United States: The Henry Doorly Zoo in Omaha (October 16, 2020) and the National Aquarium in Baltimore (November 14, 2020)" and labelled by the Roboflow team (with some help from SageMaker Ground Truth). No third-party author exists per image. The IMG_* file names do not record which of the two venues an image came from.
- Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . "Images and annotations are released under a Creative Commons By-Attribution license ... provided you give acknowledgement of their source."
- Canonical URL: https://public.roboflow.com/object-detection/aquarium (Universe mirror: https://universe.roboflow.com/brad-dwyer/aquarium-combined, published under the Roboflow account "brad-dwyer").
- Citation: Roboflow, Aquarium Dataset, 2020, CC BY 4.0, https://public.roboflow.com/object-detection/aquarium
- Verified 2026-10-21 from Internet Archive snapshots of the dataset page (https://web.archive.org/web/20231208221635/https://public.roboflow.com/object-detection/aquarium and https://web.archive.org/web/20250316102712/https://universe.roboflow.com/brad-dwyer/aquarium-combined); the live pages block automated fetches. Matches the NOTICE of reefsupport/marine-data v1.1.0 (config `fish-boxes`).
- Changes: files are taken unchanged from the `aquarium_pretrain` export (Roboflow `.rf.<hash>` file names) as redistributed in reefsupport/marine-data (https://huggingface.co/datasets/reefsupport/marine-data); this fixture adds only the split map and a box parquet.
